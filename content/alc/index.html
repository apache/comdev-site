The Apache Local Community (ALC) program brings together local groups of Apache and open source enthusiasts — called ALC Chapters — in towns and cities around the world. Chapters host local events, spread awareness of the ASF and the Apache Way, and connect project users and developers in their region.

ALC is to the ASF what Google Developer Groups, Facebook Developer Circles, and Mozilla Reps are to those organizations: a grassroots, local, in-person complement to our global online communities.

Don’t see a chapter in your town or city? You can start one. There is a single ALC chapter per town or city, so check the map first.

What a chapter does ¶

  • Spreads awareness of Apache and open source in the local community.
  • Hosts events for students, developers, and the local business community.
  • Shares knowledge about the ASF, the Apache Way, and Apache projects.
  • Brings project users and developers together, face to face.

Get involved ¶

  • Attend an event hosted by your nearest chapter.
  • Organize — start a chapter in your town, or help run an existing one (see our organizer guide).
  • Speak — chapters are always looking for speakers. Our speaker resources and sample presentations can help.

Contact ¶

  • Email — write to dev@community.apache.org with [ALC] at the start of the subject line.
  • Slack — join the #alc channel on the ASF Slack.

The authoritative program documentation — including the formal application and dissolution processes and the ALC resources library — lives on the ALC wiki.
