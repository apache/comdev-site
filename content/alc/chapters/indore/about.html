This wiki is used for ALC Indore Chapter.

Indore is a city in west-central India. Indore has been ranked as India’s cleanest city 6 years in a row as per the Swachh Survekshan for the years 2017 to 2022.

For more details, please refer https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indore

Contact Us ¶

If you have any thoughts and query to discuss with ALC Indore, please contact us with following channels.

Email - Send a mail to dev@community.apache.org with [ALC Indore] prefix in subject line.

Slack (for quick chat) - Please create a Slack account using this invite link and join the #alc channel.
