This is a place for collecting information about GSoC activities each year for historical tracking. It was started in 2012 so is missing plenty of detail in the early years. Your help filling it out would be appreciated.

Figures for 2005-2008 and 2016 onward come from Google’s GSoC archives; those for 2016-2021 list only projects that completed, so the “accepted” number isn’t known. Some older figures here came from ASF records and may differ from Google’s (2017: 23 in Google’s archive; 2018: 23). No archive survives for 2009-2015.
