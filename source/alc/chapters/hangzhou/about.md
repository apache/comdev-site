This wiki is used for ALC Hangzhou Chapter.

**Hangzhou** is the capital and most populous city of [Zhejiang](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zhejiang), [China](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/China). It is located in the northeastern part of the province, sitting at the head of [Hangzhou Bay](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hangzhou_Bay), which separates [Shanghai](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shanghai) and [Ningbo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ningbo).

For more details, please refer <https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hangzhou>
