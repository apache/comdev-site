ALC Beijing is an active chapter in the People's Republic of China, running local events and maintaining translation and special-interest-group efforts around Apache projects.

See the [chapter's wiki page](https://cwiki.apache.org/confluence/display/COMDEV/ALC+Beijing) for current organizers, upcoming
events, and how to get involved locally. To reach the chapter, email
[dev@community.apache.org](mailto:dev@community.apache.org) with `[ALC]`
and the city name in the subject line.
