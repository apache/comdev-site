会议议题：

1\. 同步TGO鲲鹏会，以及CSDN 承办会议的情况

2\. CoC Asia 整体的会议规划

本次会议主要讨论了活动的赞助商寻找进展，明确了后续需在春节前确定会议时间，并探讨了Snowflake、CSDN等潜在赞助商。  
小结  
​​1. 活动承办商寻找进展​​

鲲鹏会已退出会议承办。  
当前正在寻找CSDN作为新的承办商，同时也在评估DataFun的可能性。  
会议强调需要寻找能提供“兜底”的商业公司，以避免过度依赖志愿者。

​​2. 其他潜在赞助商探讨​​

​​Snowflake​​: 一位PMC成员（华星）曾主动联系，但其国内团队更侧重于海外业务推广，需进一步沟通。  
​​Flink​​: 聂励峰@白鲸开源提到曾联系过相关人员，但对方目前在中国负责其他业务。

​​3. 项目及市场动态​​

​​国内活动预算​​: 多方提到当前市场活动预算普遍收紧。

​​4. 会议后续安排​​

由于赞助商事宜尚未确定，会议时间将落后于往年约一个月，后续需要全体成员共同努力赶进度。  
下次会议计划在春节前召开。

Meeting Agenda:

1.  Synchronize the status of conference hosting by TGO Kunpeng Association and CSDN.

2.  Overall planning for the CoC Asia conference.

This meeting primarily discussed the progress in identifying event sponsors, clarified the need to finalize the conference dates before the Chinese New Year, and explored potential sponsors such as Snowflake and CSDN.

Summary

1.  Progress in Identifying Event Vendor  
    Kunpeng Association has withdrawn from hosting the conference.  
    Efforts are currently underway to secure CSDN as a new Vendor, while also evaluating the possibility of DataFun.  
    The meeting emphasized the need to find a commercial company that can provide a "safety net" to avoid over-reliance on volunteers.

2.  Exploration of Other Potential Sponsors  
    Snowflake: A PMC member (Huaxing) previously reached out proactively, but their domestic team is more focused on overseas business promotion, requiring further communication.  
    Flink: Nie Lifeng @ White Whale Open Source mentioned having contacted relevant personnel, but they are currently responsible for other business operations in China.

3.  Project and Market Updates  
    Domestic Event Budgets: Multiple parties mentioned that current market event budgets are generally tightening.

4.  Follow-Up Conference Arrangements  
    Due to the unresolved sponsor matters, the conference timeline will be delayed by approximately one month compared to previous years. Collective efforts from all members will be required to catch up on progress.  
    The next meeting is planned to be held before the Chinese New Year.

## Jan 15th, 2026, Thursday

Time: 10:00 PM (UTC+8 Beijing Time)

会议议题：  
1\. 中关村科学城提供活动场地， 开源社区活动可以申请  
2\. 上海经贸大学场地  
3\. TGO鲲鹏会申请承办会议，看看大家参会的意愿  
4\. CoC EU 会议时间和地点已公布

今天的会议总结

这是一场关于开源社区会议筹备的讨论会。本次会议主要同步了北京和上海两地会议场地的申请进展，并探讨了与昆鹏会等组织合作承办的可能性。参会者还讨论了活动时间安排、赞助资源对接以及社区长期发展的相关话题。

1、会议进程同步

中关村科学城将继续提供会议场地，并有其他场地可供申请。后续会有文档指导如何申请线下活动场地。  
昆鹏会有较大意愿承办会议，且8月8日在北京有活动，会议时间需错开。  
2、活动安排

本周六data for社区联合ALC北京在五道口附近举办meet up，目标报名200人，预计到场100人。  
社区主理人林旅强 将参与活动，他目前在群内但近期出差。  
3、COC大会安排

北京场地已确定，上海场地在经贸大学，但需提前更多时间申请。  
计划月底前确定会议时间，鲲鹏会需与集合班沟通申请。  
上海场地较小，不适合大型会议，建议提前规划。

4、赞助与支持

上海本土企业如携程、B站等可能提供赞助，但目前沟通较少。  
深圳和广州的活动赞助较容易获取，建议发掘上海活跃分支。

Meeting Agenda:

1.  Zhongguancun Science City provides event venues; open-source community events can apply.

2.  Venue at Shanghai University of International Business and Economics.

3.  TGO Kunpeng Association has applied to host the conference; feedback on attendees' willingness to participate is needed.

4.  CoC EU conference time and location have been announced.

Today’s Meeting Summary

This was a discussion on the preparation of open-source community conferences. The meeting primarily synchronized the progress of venue applications in Beijing and Shanghai and explored the possibility of collaborating with organizations such as Kunpeng Association to host the events. Participants also discussed event scheduling, sponsorship resource coordination, and topics related to the long-term development of the community.

1.  Synchronization of Meeting Progress  
    Zhongguancun Science City will continue to provide conference venues, and other venues are available for application. Documentation will be provided later to guide how to apply for offline event venues.  
    Kunpeng Association is highly willing to host the conference, and they have an event in Beijing on August 8th, so the conference dates need to be scheduled accordingly.

2.  Event Arrangements  
    This Saturday, the Data for Community, in collaboration with ALC Beijing, will host a meetup near Wudaokou, targeting 200 registrations with an expected attendance of 100.  
    Community lead Richard Lin will participate in the event. He is currently in the group but is on a business trip recently.

3.  CoC Conference Arrangements  
    The Beijing venue has been confirmed. The Shanghai venue is at the University of International Business and Economics, but applications need to be submitted well in advance.  
    Plans are to finalize the conference dates by the end of the month. Kunpeng Association needs to communicate with the assembly team to apply.  
    The Shanghai venue is relatively small and not suitable for large conferences, so early planning is recommended.

4.  Sponsorship and Support  
    Local Shanghai companies such as Ctrip and Bilibili may provide sponsorship, but communication has been limited so far.  
    Sponsorship for events in Shenzhen and Guangzhou is easier to obtain. It is recommended to explore active branches in Shanghai.
