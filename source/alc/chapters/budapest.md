ALC Budapest serves the open source community in Budapest, Hungary.

See the [chapter's wiki page](https://cwiki.apache.org/confluence/display/COMDEV/Apache+Local+Community+-+ALC) for current organizers, upcoming
events, and how to get involved locally. To reach the chapter, email
[dev@community.apache.org](mailto:dev@community.apache.org) with `[ALC]`
and the city name in the subject line.
