## Post Event Report

ALC Indore successfully organized the **webinar** on "Open-Source and ASF Awareness" on the 9th of May 2020.

We are pleased to share that an overwhelming number of **80+ people** including students and professionals registered for the event  
and **50+ people** attended the event.

We conducted the webinar via Google Meet and got registrations for the event beforehand.

The session duration was about 1 hour including Q\&A.

Overall it was an amazing experience for all of us and the event was a huge success.

## Agenda

ALC Indore is planning to organize a webinar on 'Open Source and ASF Awareness' on 9th May 2020.

### Session on the Open-Source and The Apache Software Foundation

The session will include the following topics.

Open Source

  - What is Open-Source
  - History of Open-Source
  - Open-Source Vs Closed source
  - Open-Source license

The Apache Way

  - About the Apache Software Foundation and its history
  - About the Apache Way
  - ICLA and how it should be signed to contribute in ASF
  - Apache Incubator
  - The Foundation structure
  - The projects under the Apache umbrella
  - The Mailing list and it's usage
  - How to pick a project based domain/programming language interest
