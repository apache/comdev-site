### Agenda

It will be an online session where we will have a general discussion about Open source and internship opportunities for the students.  
We will cover the following topics in the session, which will be around 1-2 hour long.

1\. Introduction to Open Source  
2\. The Importance of Open Source  
3\. Benefits of Contributing to Open Source as an Intern  
4\. Finding Open Source Projects  
5\. Getting Started with Open Source Contributions  
6\. Common Challenges & Overcoming Them  
7\. Internship Programs and Open Source Organisations  
8\. Success Stories  
9\. Legal and Ethical Considerations  
10\. Future Career Prospects  
11\. Conclusion  
12\. Q\&A Session

Speaker: Praveen Kumar Purushotham

Hosts: Priya Sharma & Aditya Sharma
