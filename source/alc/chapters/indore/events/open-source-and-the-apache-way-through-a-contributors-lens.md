## Post-event Report

The event was a huge success. We had **70+ attendees** from novice to pro-level developers, students, and others interested in Open Source technologies.

After a wonderful and insightful **1 hour** session by Aditya, we had a great **Q\&A** round which went on for another hour.  
The audience seemed to enjoy every bit of the knowledge transfer which resulted in the best 2-way communication in a session we have had so far.
