### Agenda

This session is targeted towards college students in Indore, to spread awareness about **Apache Kafka** and guide them through the terms and technicalities of the project to facilitate participation in the ASF projects.  
We aspire to bring in more contributions to the Apache projects by developing interest and awareness in the college students.

This will be an in-person live interactive session scheduled on 12th May 2023, Friday.

We will be covering the following topics:

  - Introduction to Apache Kafka
  - Brief History and Use Cases
  - Kafka Event and Event Streaming
  - Kafka Topics and Partitions
  - Console Source and Sink Connector
  - File Source and Sink Connector
  - Producers and Consumers

(Speaker: Ravi Lodhi)
