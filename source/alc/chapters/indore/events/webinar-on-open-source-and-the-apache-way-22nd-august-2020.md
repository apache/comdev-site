## Post Event Report

The ALC Indore successfully organized a webinar on "Open Source and The Apache Way" Session at Indore on 22nd August 2020 for Acropolis, Indore.

The session was attended by approximately 15 people including students and faculty members.  
We also motivated them to participate in the open-source hackathons and leverage the open-source internship programs like Outreachy for better learning opportunities.  
  
We conducted the webinar via Google Meet and it was a 1-hour session.  
Here is the presentation used in the webinar.

## Agenda

ALC Indore is planning to organize a second webinar on 'Open Source and ASF Awareness' on 22nd August 2020 for the undergraduates at [Acropolis](http://acropolis.in/), Indore.

In these sessions, our main emphasis is imparting knowledge about Open Source and the Apache Software Foundation to the students.  
So as to benefit them in building their skillset by encouraging them to get involved in Open Source projects under Apache.

### Session on the Open-Source and The Apache Software Foundation

Aditya Sharma and Priya Sharma will be the speaker for the session and talk about the following topics -

Open Source & The Apache Way

  - What is Open-Source
  - History of Open-Source
  - Open-Source Vs Closed source
  - Open-Source license
  - About the Apache Software Foundation and its history
  - About the Apache Way
  - ICLA and how it should be signed to contribute in ASF
  - Apache Incubator
  - The Foundation structure
  - The projects under the Apache umbrella
  - The Mailing list and it's usage
  - How to pick a project based domain/programming language interest
