Agenda

ALC Indore is planning to organize the 'Open Source and ASF Awareness' event at CDAC Authorized Training Centre ([ORLANDO ACADEMY](http://www.orlando.co.in/)) at Indore, India.

We are working on finalizing the date.

Our audience will be the engineering graduates undergoing training in advanced computing and are about to start their career in the Software Industry.  
In the event, we will have a session on Open-Source and The Apache Software Foundation (Duration \~1 hour).

We are hopeful that this session and information regarding the open-source world will empower them to make better career decisions and choose the future course of action.  
And also motivate them to contribute to the Open source community.

### Session on the Open-Source and The Apache Apache Software Foundation

The session will include the following topics.

  - What is Open source software?
  - History of Open source and ASF
  - Comparison with closed source
  - Why you should know Open Source?
  - Why you should contribute to Open Source?
  - How can you contribute?
  - Open Source License
  - Examples Open source and project under ASF umbrella.
  - Apache Software Foundation History & Projects
  - How to pick a project based domain/programming language interest from ASF
