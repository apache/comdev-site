## Agenda

ALC Indore is organizing 'The Apache Day' event for student community at [Prestige Institute of Engineering Management & Research](https://www.piemr.edu.in/) institute on 28 September 2019 in Indore, India.

The event is planned into two parts.

1.  Session on Open-Source and The Apache Way (Duration 1 - 1.5 hours)
2.  Workshop (work with 8 students for the whole day and help them to make their first contribution to Apache project)

### 1\. Session on the Open-Source and The Apache Way

A session is delivered in the morning to all the students of Computer Science and IT stream. It will include following topics.

  - What is Open-Source
  - History of Open-Source
  - Open-Source Vs Closed source
  - Open-Source license
  - About the Apache Software Foundation and it's history
  - About the Apache Way
  - What is ICLA and how it should be signed to contribute in ASF
  - Apache Incubator
  - The Foundation structure
  - The projects under Apache umbrella
  - Mailing list and it's usage
  - How to pick a project based domain/programming language interest

### 2\. Workshop

This is the next interesting and important part of the event, after the session, our volunteers with work with 8 students for whole day to make their first real contribution (patch) to Apache project.  
These students will work on issues from Apache OFBiz or Apache Roller project.

Q. Why we choose Apache OFBiz and Roller project?  
ALC Indore is having volunteers, who are committer and member of PMC in OFBiz and Roller project. Thus they will be able to provide their best mentorship on these project.  
The ALC Indore team is prepared a list of low hanging issues from both the projects on which students can work.

Q. Why only 8 Students for workshop?  
Since, it's our first event of this type, we are also learning.  
Our plan is, one volunteer will spend whole day with one students, in this way the student will get the individual focused attention.

## Post Event Report

The ACL Indore successfully organized 'The Apache Day' event in Indore on 28 September 2019.

The clicks from event are available at <https://www.flickr.com/photos/alcindore/sets/72157711347589168/>

  - **150+** Students participated in the event and attended the session on 'Open Source and The Apache Way'.

  - The duration for session was **1 hour 30 minutes** including the interaction with audience.

  - The mentors from ALC Indore team work with **8** **students** for the whole day and help them to make their first real contribution (patch) to Apache project.
