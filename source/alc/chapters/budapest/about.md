This wiki is used for [Apache Local Community](/alc/about.html) - **ALC Budapest** **Chapter**.

Budapest is an Alpha − global city with strengths in commerce, finance, media, art, fashion, research, technology, education, and entertainment.  
It is Hungary's financial center.

For more details, please refer <https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Budapest>

## Contact Us

If you have any thoughts and queries to discuss with ALC Budapest, please contact us with the following channels.

Email - Send a mail to <dev@community.apache.org> with **\[ALC Budapest\]** prefix in the subject line.

Slack (for quick chat) - Please create a Slack account using [this invite link](https://s.apache.org/slack-invite) and [join the **\#alc**](https://s.apache.org/alc-slack-channel)
