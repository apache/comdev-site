ALC Shanghai hosts events for the open source community in Shanghai, China, welcoming students, developers, and the local business community to learn about the ASF and the Apache Way.

See the [chapter's wiki page](https://cwiki.apache.org/confluence/display/COMDEV/ALC+Shanghai) for current organizers, upcoming
events, and how to get involved locally. To reach the chapter, email
[dev@community.apache.org](mailto:dev@community.apache.org) with `[ALC]`
and the city name in the subject line.
