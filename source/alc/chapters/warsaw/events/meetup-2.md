### Agenda

1.  **Apache Software Foundation values** (Jarek Potiuk, Apache Airflow PMC, Principal Software Engineer at Polidea) 15 min  
    A short introduction to one of Apache Software Foundation values. Apache Software Foundation is defined by [Apache Way](https://www.apache.org/theapacheway/). At every meeting, we discuss one of the values/aspects of Apache Way. The one chosen for the meeting is marked in bold.

    1.  **Earned Authority**
    2.  Community of Peers
    3.  Open Communications
    4.  Consensus Decision Making
    5.  Responsible Oversight
    6.  Independence
    7.  Community Over Code

2.  **Recruiting to the Open Source projects – What to remember about?** (Justyna Grzywaczyk, the People & Culture Lead at Polidea) 30 min

    Justyna is a People & Culture lead at Polidea. She takes care of Employer Branding activities, measuring and shaping and Employee Experience, organizing Social Life events, and, most importantly, hiring to all Polidea’s teams – including the Open Source. In the last year and a half, she took an active part in hiring and onboarding of over 30 new joiners in Polidea. In the course of the last year, through countless discussions, successes and failures, we’ve learned a lot about creating the effective, fair, and transparent recruitment process (that is still constantly evolving). This presentation will be the summary of good practices our team has developed.
