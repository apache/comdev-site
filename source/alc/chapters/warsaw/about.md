This wiki is used for [Apache Local Community](/alc/about.html) - **ALC Warsaw** **Chapter**.

Warsaw is the capital of Poland. Warsaw is an alpha global city a major international tourist destination, and a significant cultural, political and economic hub.  
Its historical old town was designated a UNESCO World Heritage Site.

For more details, please refer <https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Warsaw>

## Contact Us

If you have any thoughts and queries to discuss with ALC Warsaw, please contact us with following channels.

Email - Send a mail to <dev@community.apache.org> with **\[ALC Warsaw\]** prefix in the subject line.

Slack (for quick chat) - Please create a Slack account using [this invite link](https://s.apache.org/slack-invite) and [join the **\#alc**](https://s.apache.org/alc-slack-channel) and **[\#alc-warsaw](https://s.apache.org/alc-warsaw-all)** channels.

Join [ALC Warsaw](https://www.meetup.com/pl-PL/alc-warsaw/) group on meetup.com.
