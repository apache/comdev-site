ALC Lagos brings the Apache community to Lagos, Nigeria, hosting events for the growing West African open source ecosystem.

See the [chapter's wiki page](https://cwiki.apache.org/confluence/display/COMDEV/ALC+Lagos) for current organizers, events, and
reports. To reach the chapter, email
[dev@community.apache.org](mailto:dev@community.apache.org) with `[ALC]`
and the city name in the subject line.
