ALC Islamabad was approved in September 2026 and is getting started with
organizing its first meetups in the Islamabad open source community.

See the [chapter's wiki page](https://cwiki.apache.org/confluence/display/COMDEV/ALC+Islamabad)
for current organizers, events, and reports. To reach the chapter, email
[dev@community.apache.org](mailto:dev@community.apache.org) with `[ALC]`
in the subject line.
