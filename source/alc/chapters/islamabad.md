ALC Islamabad was approved in September 2026. It is led by Alina Kazi,
with Pushmina Kazi as co-lead, and is getting started with organizing its
first meetups in the Islamabad open source community.

See the [chapter's wiki page](https://cwiki.apache.org/confluence/spaces/COMDEV/pages/451974627/ALC+Islamabad)
for current organizers, events, and reports. To reach the chapter, email
[dev@community.apache.org](mailto:dev@community.apache.org) with `[ALC]`
in the subject line.
