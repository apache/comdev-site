This wiki is used for ALC Xi'an Chapter.

Xi'an is the capital of [Shaanxi Province](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shaanxi). A [subprovincial city](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sub-provincial_division#Sub-provincial_municipalities) on the [Guanzhong Plain](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Guanzhong),<sup>[\[6\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Xi%27an#cite_note-6)</sup> the city is the third most populous city in [Western China](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Western_China), after [Chongqing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chongqing) and [Chengdu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chengdu), as well as the most populous city in [Northwest China](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Northwest_China).

For more details, please refer <https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Xi%27an>
