Status: **\[PROPOSAL\]**

Event date: 27th March 2024 (Wednesday), 7:00 to 8:00 pm

"First Contributions" will be an online panel discussion marking ALC Bangalore's first community event. The idea is to have ALC Bangalore members share their experiences contributing to ASF projects. The session will appeal more broadly outside the ASF/ASF project-specific communities.

We can use the following prompts for the session:

1.  What was your first pull request? What's the story behind it?
2.  Was that your first open source contribution?
3.  What advice would you give to first-time contributors?
4.  ...
