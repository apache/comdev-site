This wiki is used for ALC Bangalore Chapter.

**Bangalore** [officially](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_renamed_places_in_India) **Bengaluru**, is the [capital](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Capital_city) and largest city of the [southern Indian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/South_India) [state](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indian_state) of [Karnataka](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Karnataka). Bangalore is known as the "Silicon Valley of India" because it is the nation's leading [software](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Software) exporter as well as a major semiconductor hub.

For more details, please refer <https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bangalore>
