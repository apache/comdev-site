This wiki is used for ALC Shenzhen Chapter.

Shenzhen is a major sub-provincial city and one of the special economic zones of China. Shenzhen is a global center in technology, research, manufacturing, finance, and transportation, and the Port of Shenzhen is the world's fourth busiest container port. For more details, please refer to <https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shenzhen>
