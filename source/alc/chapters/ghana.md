ALC Ghana is a proposed chapter put forward in August 2026 by Ezra Yendau
(based in Wa, Ghana), with Seth Mensah and Julius Boakye. The proposal
aims to bring open source mentorship, technical workshops, and
collaboration with local universities and tech communities to the region,
with Rich Bowen mentoring. As of early October 2026 the proposal was
moving toward approval on the ComDev list but had not been formally
finalized.

> **Status note:** The request was framed as a country-wide "ALC Ghana"
> rather than a single town or city. ALC is organized one chapter per
> town/city, so the exact city (the lead is based in Wa; Accra is the
> capital) should be confirmed with the organizers before this page is
> finalized.
