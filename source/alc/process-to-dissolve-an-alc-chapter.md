---
title: "Process to dissolve an ALC Chapter"
wiki_source: https://cwiki.apache.org/confluence/pages/viewpage.action?pageId=138022155
wiki_last_modified: 2019-12-26
---

If an ALC Chapter is inactive for 3 months (or any reason including lack of reporting, no longer having required guidance, event branding issues, etc.), it will be dissolved after communication with the ALC chapter members because we are having strictly one ALC chapter in a town/city.

1\. The [ALC Chair](/alc/roles-and-responsibilities-in-managing-alc-initiative.html#alc-chair) will make sure quarterly report is filed by ALC Chapter.

2\. In case of failure of filing the report, the [ALC Chair](/alc/roles-and-responsibilities-in-managing-alc-initiative.html#alc-chair) will have communication with ALC chapter members (by keeping ComDev PMC in the loop) to understand the actual situation.

If the ALC Chapter is no longer interested in participating in ALC activities (or any reason including lack of reporting, no longer having required guidance, event branding issues, etc.), the ALC Chapter will be dissolved by **ComDev PMC VOTE**.

3\. The [ALC Chair](/alc/roles-and-responsibilities-in-managing-alc-initiative.html#alc-chair) will mark as an archive to all the wiki documented (mentioned in point 4 of [Process to establish an ALC Chapter](/alc/process-to-establish-an-alc-chapter.html)) created for that ALC Chapter.

4\. ComDev PMC member will send an announcement mail of 'dissolution of the respective ALC Chapter' on <announce@apache.org> (keeping <dev@community.apache.org> in CC).
