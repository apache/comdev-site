The **Apache Local Community (ALC)** program brings together local groups
of Apache and open source enthusiasts — called *ALC Chapters* — in towns
and cities around the world. Chapters host local events, spread awareness
of the ASF and the Apache Way, and connect project users and developers
in their region.

ALC is to the ASF what Google Developer Groups, Facebook Developer
Circles, and Mozilla Reps are to those organizations: a grassroots,
local, in-person complement to our global online communities.

[start one](/alc/start-a-chapter.html). There is a single ALC chapter per
town or city, so check the map first.

## What a chapter does

* Spreads awareness of Apache and open source in the local community.
* Hosts events for students, developers, and the local business community.
* Shares knowledge about the ASF, the Apache Way, and Apache projects.
* Brings project users and developers together, face to face.

## Get involved

* **Attend** an event hosted by your nearest chapter.
* **Organize** — [start a chapter](/alc/start-a-chapter.html) in your town,
  or help run an existing one (see our [organizer guide](/alc/organizers.html)).
* **Speak** — chapters are always looking for speakers. Our
  [speaker resources](/speakers/) and [sample presentations](/speakers/slides.html)
  can help.

## Contact

* **Email** — write to [dev@community.apache.org](mailto:dev@community.apache.org)
  with `[ALC]` at the start of the subject line.
* **Slack** — join the `#alc` channel on
  [the ASF Slack](https://infra.apache.org/slack.html).

The authoritative program documentation — including the formal
application and dissolution processes and the ALC resources library —
lives on the
[ALC wiki](https://cwiki.apache.org/confluence/display/COMDEV/Apache+Local+Community+-+ALC).
