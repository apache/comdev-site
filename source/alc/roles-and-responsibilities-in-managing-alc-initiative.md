## ALC Chair

The ALC Chair will be responsible for ensuring:

1.  The quarterly report is filed by all the ALC Chapters.
2.  Pre-event materials are reviewed with the help of PMC.
3.  ComDev PMC will reach out to ALC Chair for $reasons.

## ALC Chapter Lead

Each ALC Chapter will have one Chapter Lead and will have the following roles and responsibilities.

1.  Making sure the [ALC Code of Conduct](https://s.apache.org/alc-code-of-conduct) is followed by their ALC Chapter.
2.  All the events organized by the ALC Chapter are following the [Guidelines to organise ALC Event](/alc/alc-resources/guidelines-to-organise-alc-event.html).
3.  Submit the [status report](/alc/reports.html) to [ComDev](mailto:dev@community.apache.org) (mailto:dev@community.apache.org) in every three months based on their reporting cycle.  
    (keep your other ALC Chapter members in CC.)
4.  The Chapter activities are in line with [ALC Roles and Responsibilities](/alc/about.html).
5.  Chapter Lead will be the point of contact for the respective ALC Chapter.

## ALC Chapter Member

1.  Making sure the [ALC Code of Conduct](https://s.apache.org/alc-code-of-conduct) is followed by ALC Chapter.
2.  Making sure all the events organized by the ALC Chapter are following the [Guidelines to organise ALC Event](/alc/alc-resources/guidelines-to-organise-alc-event.html).
3.  Contribute to activities that are in line with [ALC Roles and Responsibilities](/alc/about.html).
4.  Help in preparing the [status report](/alc/reports.html) to [ComDev](mailto:dev@community.apache.org) in every three months based on their reporting cycle.
5.  As the Chapter Members have edit rights to Confluence, keep updated all the information available in the Chapter's space, like [Reports](/alc/guidelines-to-manage-an-alc-chapter.html), respective events detail pages.
6.  Maintain the [ALC Resources](/alc/alc-resources/) document.
