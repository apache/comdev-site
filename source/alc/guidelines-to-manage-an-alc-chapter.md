## Adding a new member to ALC Chapter

The ALC Chapter is responsible for adding new members to that ALC Chapters.  
  
If the interested person is an ASF Committer, PMC member, or ASF Member, they can be directly added to the ALC chapter. In this case, simply send an update email to the private list at <private@community.apache.org> informing them about the new addition.

However, if the interested volunteer is not an ASF Committer, PMC member, or ASF Member, an existing chapter member should initiate an internal vote among the current chapter members. This vote email should include <private@community.apache.org> in CC.

Once the vote is passed, the result can be shared on the <dev@community.apache.org> list. You can also share the new member’s Confluence ID there so appropriate edit permissions can be granted.

> **Info:** There is no bar of committership to be a member of an ALC Chapter.  
> As per the ALC guidelines, it is mandatory to have at least 1 ASF member plus 2 PMC members in the ALC Chapter team as mentioned at  
> <https://s.apache.org/establish-alc-chapter>  
> The rest members need not be necessarily committers, they can be non-committers.

Sample mail:  
Subject line: \[ALC \<chapter-name\>\] \[Vote\] Invite \<invitee-member-name\> as ALC \<chapter-name\> member

```
Hello ALC <chapter-name> members,

<invitee-member-name> has shown his interest in joining the ALC <chapter-name> Chapter.

<Add details about invitee-member>

e.g.
As invitee-member-name is an active contributor to the Apache OFBiz project
and understands the Apache Way well, I propose to invite him to join
the ALC <chapter-name>.

Please vote:

+1 to invite
-1 with a reason not to invite

The vote will be open for 7 days.

########
@ALC <chapter-name> team,
We are following the guidelines mentioned at
https://s.apache.org/alc-member-invite for inviting new chapter
members. Please refer to it for more details.
As per the guidelines keeping the private list
(private@community.apache.org) in CC to keep them updated.
```

## How to change the Chapter Lead

If a Chapter wishes to change their Chapter Lead for any reason, the ALC Chapter member can initiate a discussion on it internally among the existing chapter members (keeping the private list - <private@community.apache.org> in CC). As per the interest of volunteer, current chapter lead or existing chapter member can initiate the Vote internally among the existing chapter members to nominate new Chapter Lead.  
Once the vote is passed, the updates can be shared on <dev@community.apache.org> list.
