## The following process will be followed to establish an ALC Chapter. Step 1:

1\. A volunteer will request to establish an ALC Chapter by sending a message to <dev@community.apache.org> with "\[ALC\] Request to establish ALC in \<name-of-your-town-or-city\>" in the subject line.  
Before sending the request, the volunteer needs to make sure the following points are addressed

1.1 There will be a single ALC chapter per town/city, so before sending the request please check the [ALC Chapters](/alc/chapters/about.html) to see if there’s an existing chapter in your location.

1.2 It is mandatory to have at least **1 ASF** member plus **2 PMC** members in the ALC Chapter team. The volunteer needs to make sure the proposed ALC chapter team meets this criterion.

> **Note:**
>
> 1.  If a proposed ALC Chapter is not having any ASF member on a **case-by-case basis** ComDev PMC may assign a mentor (who will be an ASF member) to the proposed ALC Chapter and establish it.
>
> 2.  We will revisit the requirement of having at least one ASF member plus two PMC members in the next six months (June 2020). By that time, our community will have gained more experience with how ALC Chapters are performing, identified areas for improvement, and recognized what we are doing well. Based on these insights, we will reconsider this clause.
>
> Reference for the above discussion - <https://s.apache.org/lio8r>

> **Info:** ComDev PMC may provide flexibility and mentorship for those parts of the world where there are no local members/PMC members/committers, since any proposed ALC from those regions represents a clear opportunity to expand our footprint into new places.

-----

## Step 2:

2\. After receiving a message on <dev@community.apache.org>, a ComDev PMC member starts a thread with the interested members. We first confirm a few key details with the ALC participants.  
The purpose is to ensure that **all members agree to the roles and responsibilities associated with the ALC**, that they support the proposed Chapter Lead, and that the **Chapter Lead accepts their responsibilities as well**.

Here’s a sample email for reference: <https://lists.apache.org/thread/5h4wn3r3no8ty77xvombwd5pco6kfmr2>

This email is sent to **all interested members, with <private@community.apache.org> copied in CC**.

**This vote remains open for 7 days.**

## Step 3:

3\. If we receive significant support in the above thread from interested member then a ComDev PMC member will start a thread on the **private** list to discuss on ALC Chapter establish proposal and include a link to the original vote thread—like this:  
<https://lists.apache.org/thread/1ly7xvp1vw70p8vwosqbql5bt3jgglkh>

## Step 4:

4\. Based on discussions and votes, the PMC will decide on the establishment of the ALC Chapter. This decision will be shared in the original thread initiated by the volunteer. If, for any reason, the request to establish an ALC Chapter is rejected, the PMC may provide the reasons for rejection on a case-by-case basis.

-----

## Step 5:

5\. As the PMC **approves** the request to establish the ALC Chapter, the PMC will take the following actions.

5.1 Create the following "**placeholder"** pages for the ALC Chapter in [Apache Local Community - ALC](/alc/about.html) space.

5.1.1 Create a child page of [ALC Chapters](/alc/chapters/about.html) with the title as "ALC \<name-of-alc\>". e.g. [ALC Indore](/alc/chapters/indore/about.html)  
This page will contain details about ALC Chapters and its members.

5.1.2 Create three child pages under the recently created "ALC \<name-of-alc\>" page (i.e. in the page created at 4.1.2).  
These three pages are as follows:  
  
5.1.2.1 ALC \<name-of-alc\> Events e.g. [ALC Indore Events](/alc/chapters/indore/events/)  
This page will contain the details of past and upcoming events by the ALC Chapter.  
  
5.1.2.2 ALC \<name-of-alc\> Reports e.g. [ALC Indore Reports](/alc/chapters/indore/reports/)  
This page will contain the ALC Chapter Reports.

5.1.2.3 ALC \<name-of-alc\> Team meeting [ALC Indore Team meeting](/alc/chapters/indore/team-meeting.html)  
This page will contain the ALC Chapter Meeting Notes.

5.2 Add an entry of the ALC Chapter in [Reports](/alc/reports.html).  
  
5.3 Provide the edit rights to the ComDev wiki to ALC Chapter volunteer.

-----

## Step 6:

6\. PMC member will share details of the above-created pages and will request ALC Chapter member to maintain them on the same mail thread (which was initiated by the volunteer for requesting to establish an ALC Chapter).  
The volunteer who initiated the request to be set up the ALC Chapter will become the **[Chapter Lead](/alc/roles-and-responsibilities-in-managing-alc-initiative.html#alc-chapter-lead)** for that ALC Chapter. And will make sure that the respective ALC Chapter is following [ALC Code of Conduct](https://s.apache.org/alc-code-of-conduct) and [Guidelines to organise ALC Event](/alc/alc-resources/guidelines-to-organise-alc-event.html).

Sample mail to respond:

```
Hello ALC <chapter-name> team,

Hope you are doing well, it gives immense pleasure to us to update you
that the vote is passed by ComDev PMC to establish ALC <chapter-name> Chapter
and <chapter-lead-name> as ALC <chapter-name> Chapter lead.

Here are the placeholder pages created in ComDev space
https://s.apache.org/alc-<chapter-name>
https://s.apache.org/alc-<chapter-name>-events
https://s.apache.org/alc-<chapter-name>-reports
https://s.apache.org/alc-<chapter-name>-team-meeting

I have given edit rights on Confluence for ComDev wiki to following
members (since I found their confluence IDs)
-- <chapter-member-name>
-- <chapter-member-name>
-- <chapter-member-name>
-- ...

Rest members, please feel free to mail on the dev list to request the
edit rights with your confluence ID.

We would also like to connect you with <alc-chapter-mentor-name>.
<alc-chapter-mentor-name> will be with you for an initial few months
to support and assist you so that you will be familiar 
with the processes followed by ALC Chapter for its
execution.

Dear <chapter-lead-name> and team,
We have the following roles and responsibilities as ALC Chapter and
ALC Chapter lead:
[Online version of this is available at
https://s.apache.org/ALC-Chapter-Lead-RR ]

 1. Making sure the 'ALC Code of Conduct (
https://s.apache.org/alc-code-of-conduct )' is followed by the ALC
Chapter.

 2. All the events organized by the ALC Chapter are following the
Guidelines to organize ALC Event ( https://s.apache.org/alc-guidelines ).

 3. Chapter lead will submit the status report (
https://s.apache.org/alc-reports ) to ComDev (
dev@community.apache.org ) in every *three months* based on their
reporting cycle.
For ALC <chapter-name> the reporting cycle is <alc-chapter-reporting-cycle>.

 4. Chapter Lead will be the point of contact for the respective ALC Chapter.

If you have any queries or questions, please feel free to post on dev list.

Also, here is a link to ALC Resources, it contains the information and resources
(presentations, media, etc.) that will be helpful for ALC chapters.
https://s.apache.org/alc-resources

You can also find the base ALC logos here: https://s.apache.org/alc-branding-materials

All the very best team, and thank you for showing your kind interest
in this initiative.
```

**Subject line: \[ANNOUNCE\] - Establishing ALC \<chapter-name\> Chapter**

```

ALC Chapter - ALC <chapter-name> [2] and <chapter-lead-name> as ALC <chapter-name> Chapter
lead.

1. <chapter-member-name>
-- ASF Member
-- Chair, Servicecomb
-- PMC (Camel, CXF, Incubator, RocketMQ, ServiceMix, SkyWalking)

2. <chapter-member-name>
-- ASF Member
-- PMC (Brooklyn, Camel, Incubator, TinkerPop)
-- Non-PMC (Fundraising)

3. <chapter-member-name>

4. <chapter-member-name>
-- Committer (Airflow)

luck for their future events.

[1] https://s.apache.org/alc
[2] https://s.apache.org/alc-<chapter-name>

Best regards,
The Apache ComDev team,
community.apache.org
```
