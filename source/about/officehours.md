# Office hours

ComDev participants meet informally for 'office hours' to discuss progress and blockers, identify opportunities for improving our understanding of community-building, and keep up with each other's successes and challenges. Anyone with questions or suggestions related to ASF community-building is welcome to join the conversation.

In 2026, office hours are on **Wednesdays at 1400 UTC**, on the `comdev` channel in the <a href="https://the-asf.slack.com/" target="_blank">the-asf</a> space on **Slack**. Log in to Slack using your ASF email address, if you have one.
