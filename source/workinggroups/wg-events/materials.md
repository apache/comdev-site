# Event Materials Reimbursement

Up to $200 per event for locally-purchased materials. **Pre-approval
required** before purchase.

## What's covered

- Stickers (ASF logo, project logos)
- Name badges and lanyards
- Signage (directional signs, welcome banners)
- Power strips and extension cords (if venue doesn't provide)
- Markers, sticky notes, whiteboard supplies
- Printed handouts (getting started guides, etc.)

## What's NOT covered

- T-shirts (separate ambassador program)
- Food/drinks (covered under meetup/sprint/summit budgets)
- AV equipment purchases
- Anything primarily promotional for a company

## Process

1. **Get pre-approval** (send the form below BEFORE purchasing)
2. Committee approves on-list (48hr for <$100; single confirmation
   for $100-$200)
3. Purchase materials
4. Reply to your approval thread with receipts (photos are fine)
5. Reimbursement processed within 30 days
