# Personas

The ComDev website serves a number of audiences, including:

- People arriving at the ASF and trying to find what where they might fit and what first steps to take
- Corporations seeking to understand what they are getting into, if they give employees time/funds to get involved in an ASF project
- Projects trying to understand how to grow and become stronger
- ALCs or similar, seeking info on developing and strengthening cross-project ASF groups
- (maybe) Funders and the wider open source world, looking for evidence that the ASF is vital and active

We should review both the contents and site organization to make sure our many audiences can easily find what they are looking for.
